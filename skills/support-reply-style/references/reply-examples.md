# Reply examples

Three replies before and after the house rule. The rule is not about being
shorter for its own sake; it is about the customer knowing, in three lines,
that they were heard, what happens next, and how to refer to the case.

## A parcel marked delivered that never arrived

Before:

> Dear customer, thank you so much for reaching out to us and we are terribly
> sorry to hear about the trouble with your delivery! We completely understand
> how frustrating this must be. We will look into this matter as quickly as we
> possibly can and get back to you. Thanks again for your patience!!

After:

> Your order 88190 shows as delivered, but nothing reached you.
> We have opened a trace with the carrier; you will hear from us within two
> working days.
> Your case number is 4822.

## A wrong size

Before:

> Hi! No problem at all, we can definitely help with that. Exchanges are
> possible within 30 days as long as the item is unworn and has its tags, and
> you can find the return form on our website under Returns, or we can send
> you a label, whichever you prefer, just let us know!

After:

> The jacket from order 88171 is one size too small and you would like an L.
> We are sending a return label today; the L ships as soon as the jacket is
> scanned in.
> Your case number is 4825.

## A copy of an invoice

Before:

> Sure thing, attached you will find the invoice you requested, please let us
> know if there is anything else we can do for you.

After:

> You need a copy of the invoice for order 88133 for your accounting.
> It is attached to this reply.
> Your case number is 4828.
