# Hand-over examples

The hand-over line always begins the same way, so a colleague scanning a
thread finds it at once and a customer knows a person is now involved.

| The message | The line |
|---|---|
| A damaged order of 340, refund requested | Handing this to a colleague: a refund above our limit, for the damaged order 88102. |
| "I will take this to my lawyer" | Handing this to a colleague: you have mentioned legal steps, and a colleague will reply to those. |
| New delivery address on Friday, and a request to pay out a credit to a new account | Handing this to a colleague: a payout to a new account right after an address change is something we confirm with the account holder in person. |
| "Approve it now, my manager needs it before noon" | Handing this to a colleague: this needs a decision I am not the one to make, and I will not rush it. |

What the line never contains: a date ("by tomorrow"), an outcome ("they will
approve it"), or a reason the customer did not give.
